Voice Clarity

Voice Clarity

Use ↑/↓ or Page Up/Down to scroll. Escape closes.

100% filtered audio, not 100% noise removal. Lower mixes restore original sound, including noise. Changing the mix does not turn the filter On.

If the filter stops, the original sound may return suddenly louder. Some players may not be filtered.

On shows your requested setting, not confirmation that filtering works. Audio status and failure messages are not shown automatically.

Sound is processed on your device, never uploaded. Tries to make speech clearer, not isolate a particular person. Music, sirens and quiet or radio voices may also change.

Turning it On adds about 30 ms of delay, plus any delay from your browser, even at 0% mix. Audio Sync cannot make late sound arrive earlier.

For this tab only, separate from Audio Sync. Reloading keeps settings; visiting a different page turns the filter Off and resets the mix to 100%. Turning it Off keeps your mix.

Some websites or players may not support the filter. If sound fails, turn both audio features Off and reload the page.

More information

The same shortcuts work in every tab, but change only the tab you're using. They are ignored while typing in text fields. Number-row keys can be assigned; they are not enabled by default.

Click the same shortcut again or go Back to cancel. Escape clears only the shortcut being set. A key cannot be assigned to two actions.

Quick toggle uses each tab’s last non-1× speed.

Voice Clarity mix shortcuts adjust by 10 percentage points (50% → 60%), within 0–100%, without turning the filter On. Both audio toggles retain their selected delay or mix.
